A single one-line, one-star review written by a houseplant about the human who owns it — under 280 characters, with a sharp plant's-eye grievance at its centre.

## Entry

> One star: he waters me with leftover iced coffee, tells his dates I am 'low-maintenance,' then drags my pot into direct sun like a punishment and asks why I look stressed.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node stdlib only) reads `entry.txt`, validates it against the gate — UTF-8 text, 1 to 279 characters, not only whitespace, a single line — and writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
```
